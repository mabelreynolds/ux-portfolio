About Me

Hi, I'm Mabel.

I'm an Information Science student at the University of Wisconsin-Madison pursuing a career in UX design. I'm especially interested in understanding how design choices can shape the way people interact with and experience technology and the relationship between people and design.

My background in dance has shaped the way I approach both creative and professional work. Through years of training and performing, I've grown an appreciation for creativity, collaboration, and expressing ideas in meaningful ways. After transitioning into Information Science, I discovered a new way to combine my creative interests with problem-solving and technology.

My Approach to Design

My experience in dance has shaped the way I approach design. Both require creativity, attention to detail, and an understanding of how people experience and interact with their surroundings.

As I continue developing my UX skills, I'm particularly interested in user-centered design, accessibility, and finding creative solutions to everyday challenges.

What I'm Currently Learning

Through my Information Science coursework, I'm gaining experience with UX research, task flows, wireframing, usability evaluation, and web development using HTML and CSS.

I'm also beginning to explore Figma as part of my mobile app design coursework. I'm excited to continue building these skills through hands-on experience.
